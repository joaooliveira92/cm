# 01: Does the post-match press conference overturn Group M's v1 exclusion, and how far?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

Group M's [ticket 02](../../group-m-media-press-and-communications/issues/02-v1-scope.md) kept all
media out of v1. The human now wants the manager to face journalists right after accepting a match
result. How much of that exclusion is reversed, what may the conference touch (world state, Continue,
the save, the News Inbox, Manager Pillars), and where do its questions come from?

## Answer

**Only the post-match press conference enters v1, as flavour: automatic after Accept, never blocking
Continue, deterministic templated questions with three-tone answers, recorded as one match-stream
event read back through the News Inbox.** See [Agent Note](../../../.agents/notes/proposed/feature/2026-10-06-post-match-press-conference-enters-v1.md).
