# 04: How does the press conference look and sit on Match day?

Type: prototype
Blocked by: 01

## Question

Where does the panel appear once the result is accepted (replacing the "Result accepted" line,
beside the Post-match Summary, or as an overlay on Match day), how is one question presented with
its three answers, how does it move to the next question, and what does a finished conference leave
on screen? It must leave Continue visible and enabled throughout, be fully keyboard-operable, and
read in the shipped type scale. Resolved by a throwaway prototype the human reacts to.
