# 02: What do real post-match press conferences ask?

Type: research
Blocked by: None
Status: resolved

## Question

What questions do journalists put to a football manager at a post-match press conference, and what
in the match drives each? Group them by trigger the game can detect from a recorded match: result
and margin, a late or decisive goal, a scorer (brace, debut, first goal), a dismissal, an injury,
a refereeing decision, League position or a run of form, the opponent's standing. For each group,
note the typical answer registers managers use (praise, deflection, criticism, humour). Primary
sources: published transcripts and club or league press-conference write-ups. Authors no game copy
and no counts; this grounds ticket 03.

Output: one research note under `docs/research/`.

## Answer

**Eight detectable trigger groups (result and margin, late or decisive goal, scorer milestones,
dismissals, injury, refereeing decisions, League position and form, opponent standing) and twelve
answer registers, with the topics no match record can trigger listed separately.** Findings, not a
decision, so no Agent Note: see [the research note](../../../docs/research/post-match-press-conference-questions.md),
including its Gaps section (question wording is rarely published verbatim; no source gives trigger
frequencies; coverage leans on the English top flight).
