Type: prototype
Blocked by: 01, 02, 04

## Question

What does the shared layer look like once it carries the CM look? Prototype, in a detached worktree,
the CM colour tokens replacing the palette-driven role tokens, the restyled shadcn primitives the
inventory shows are most used (Button, Select, Table, Tabs, Dialog, Card, Badge, inputs and
checkboxes), `theme.ts`, and the app chrome (header band, sidebar, bottom bar), with
`tactics/cmChrome.ts` folded in. Show it on three representative screens chosen from the inventory
(a dense list such as Squad or Player Search, a profile, and the news inbox) beside the reference,
for the human to react to. Resolved when the human approves the foundation's look.
