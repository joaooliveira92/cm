# 13 - How much geography a results-only nation is worth

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03, 07

## Question

[03](03-world-catalogue-and-canonical-ids.md) writes city rows for every activated nation regardless of
Simulation Depth, and its note explicitly hands this question on: whether a nation nobody can see the
inside of needs its cities at all.

[07](07-simulation-depth-persistence.md) has now settled what Depth means on disk — `results-only`
clubs carry no players, and the tier is justified on recurring simulation cost rather than storage —
which sharpens this into a question 03 could not phrase. It is *not* answered by 07: cities are
world-catalogue geography, not a per-club shape, and nothing in the Depth model reads them.

- What actually reads a city row for a `results-only` nation? Club naming and birthplace are the
  candidates, and 07 removed the players that would carry a birthplace.
- Is the cost worth measuring at all? Ticket 04's units put ~60 cities per nation far below noise on
  disk, so any argument for trimming has to come from generation time or from curation effort — the
  cities are curated real geography, so each one is human work, not generated bytes.
- If cities are skipped for a `results-only` nation, what happens when that nation is later deepened,
  or when one of its clubs is promoted across the boundary and needs a conjured squad?

## Handoff from ticket 08

[08](08-player-provenance-and-nationality.md) made birthplace a nullable `birth_city_id`, with NULL
meaning "born outside the loaded world". That prices part of this ticket's question: trimming a
`results-only` nation's city rows gives every player originating from that nation a NULL birthplace.
Whether that is a cost worth paying, or invisible because nobody opens the profile of a player in a
competition they cannot see inside, is this ticket's to weigh.
