# 10: Whether line and side ratings change over a career

Type: grilling
Blocked by: 03
Status: resolved

## Question

CM 03/04 let a manager retrain a player's position or side ("Retrain Position/Side", per the
[positional research](../../../docs/research/player-positional-model-cm0304-positional-fields.md)).
Decide whether this game models that: a retraining target set per player, how fast ratings rise and
whether anything decays from disuse, and whether it belongs to Player Development at Season
conclusion or to the Training Schedule. Research first, if the answer needs CM 03/04's actual
retraining rules.

## Answer

**Retraining only: one line-or-side target per player, rising through the weekly training tick, no
decay, no growth from playing.** See [Agent Note](../../../.agents/notes/proposed/feature/2026-09-29-positions-retrain-through-training-only.md).
