## Question

Ticket 01 establishes what Free Role is in CM 03/04: a positional line, a separate numeric
attribute, a boolean editor flag, or only a tactical instruction. Decide whether this effort models
it and, if so, where it lives: in the positional model, as an Attribute, or as a tactics concept
(a per-slot instruction beside Role). Free Role is not folded into the position system unless the
research shows CM stored it there.

If it is a tactical instruction, it is out of scope here and belongs to the tactics domain; record
that and close.

## Answer

**Free Role is a twelfth 1-20 positional rating, stored with the lines and hidden from every screen;
the Free Role player instruction belongs to the formations-and-instructions effort and its effect
scales with this rating.** The staff "Free Roles" coaching preference is out of scope. See
[Agent Note](../../../.agents/notes/proposed/architecture/2026-09-29-players-store-cm-line-and-side-ratings.md).
