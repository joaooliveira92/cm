# 11: The squad position column and position filters

Type: grilling
Blocked by: 05, 08
Status: resolved

## Question

The squad, search and transfer screens show a position column, group by position and filter by it,
all over the ten Positions. Decide what each shows with the new model: the compact label as the
column, how the column sorts (pitch order of the best line?), and what a position filter matches
(a cell's suitability ≥ 15, a line, a line and side?). The Tactics screen belongs to the sibling
effort's Tactics screen ticket.

## Answer

**The compact label as the column, sorted by pitch order of the best line then side; grouping by best
natural line; filters match suitability ≥ 15; raw ratings hidden everywhere, with per-cell suitability
on the Tactics screen.** See [Agent Note](../../../.agents/notes/proposed/feature/2026-09-29-positional-ratings-stay-hidden.md).
